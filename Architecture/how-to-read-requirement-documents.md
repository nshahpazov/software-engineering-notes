# How to read requirement documents

## The 3 pass method

### Pass 1 - Orientation
- Get the big picture and the major themes
- Read quickly without too much analysis
- Jot down
    - A glossary of unclear or domain specific terms
    - Any potential architectural hotspots
- Identify
    - Who are the actors (customers, vendors, staff, etc.)
    - What the major entities or systems are
    - What does the system seem to care about

### Pass 2 - Deep read for requirement extraction

##### Goal: Extract candidate requirements, categorized for the architecture

Read sentence by sentence and identify
- Functional Requirements (FRs)
- Quality Attributes (QAs) / Non-functional requirements (NFRs)
- Constraints
- Business Goal
- LT (Long Term) Goals

### Pass 3 - Consolidation and Normalization

##### Goal: Convert raw notes into structured, unambiguous inputs for **ADD**(Attribute-Driven Design)
- Consolidate and normalize the requirements by
    - Grouping related requirements together
    - Rephrase to clear and unambiguous statements
    - Split Compound requirements into atomic ones
    - Use Actor-based functional decomposition
