# Snow Family

The AWS Snow Family consists of physical devices and capacity points that help customers transfer large amounts of data into and out of AWS. The Snow Family includes:

- **AWS Snowcone**: The smallest member of the Snow Family, designed for edge computing and data transfer in environments with limited space and power. It has a storage capacity of up to 8 TB and can be used for data collection, processing, and transfer to AWS.
- **Snowcone SSD**: An SSD storage option for the AWS Snowcone device, providing faster data access and transfer speeds compared to traditional HDD storage. 14 TB usable SSD
- **AWS Snowball**: A larger device with a storage capacity of up to 80 TB. It is used for large-scale data migrations and transfers, as well as edge computing tasks. Snowball devices are rugged and can operate in harsh environments.
- **AWS Snowmobile**: The largest member of the Snow Family, designed for exabyte-scale data transfers. It is a 45-foot long shipping container that can hold up to 100 PB of data. Snowmobile is used for massive data migrations, such as moving entire data centers to AWS.
- **Snowball Edge**: A device that combines data transfer capabilities with edge computing functionality. It comes in two variants: Snowball Edge Storage Optimized (up to 80 TB storage) and Snowball Edge Compute Optimized (up to 42 TB storage with enhanced compute capabilities). Snowball Edge devices can run AWS Lambda functions and EC2 instances at the edge.
